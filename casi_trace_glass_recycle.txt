2. **Critic Agent**  
   - **Role**: Analyzes Generator outputs for flaws, gaps, and missed opportunities.  
   - **Capabilities**:

   - Explicitly cross-references Critic’s points (e.g., "Revised section 4.2 to include Five Freedoms certification").

   - Stops when user-defined thresholds (e.g., 9/10 feasibility) are met or iterations complete.

3. **Error Handling**  
   - **LLM Failures**: Retry logic (3 attempts), fallback models, and user alerts.

4. **Scoring System**

- **Scalability Assumptions**: "Cloud-based backend" is too generic. Specify **horizontal scaling strategies** (e.g., Kubernetes, serverless functions) and **cost controls** (e.g., rate limits, budget caps).  

---

### **Final Verdict**  
The I³RS concept has strong foundations for iterative idea refinement but requires **concrete, domain-specific scaffolding** to avoid generic AI pitfalls. Without tighter constraints on prompting, scoring, and model dependencies, it risks becoming a "nice-to-have" tool rather than a mission-critical system.
